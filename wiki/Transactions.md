# Transactions

Transactions are the heart of the app. Every dollar that moves — earned or spent — is a transaction. Recording them is what keeps your balances, budgets, and analytics accurate.

## The two types

- **Expense** — money leaving an account. Subtracts from the account balance.
- **Income** — money coming in. Adds to the account balance.

## Adding a transaction

Click **Add transaction**, or press **Ctrl+N** (**⌘N** on Mac) from anywhere. Pick **Expense** or **Income** first, since it decides which categories you see. Then:

- **Amount**: the cursor starts here.
- **Category**: your eight most-used categories are shown as buttons; **More…** has the rest. Switching between expense and income picks a matching category for you.
- **Account**: starts on the account you used last.
- **Date**: **Today**, **Yesterday**, or any date.
- **Note**: optional.
- **More options**: **Payee**, **Payment method** and **Tags** (comma-separated, searchable).

Press **Enter** or click **Save**.

## Reading the list

Transactions are grouped by day: **Today**, **Yesterday**, then dates. Each day shows its net on the right. Each row shows what it was (the payee, the note, or the category), the category and account underneath, and the amount: **+** for money in, **−** for money out.

A small tag marks entries that came from somewhere else: **Split with Vali** for your share of a bill (see [[Loans]]), **Tuition** for a tuition payment (see [[Tuition]]).

The list loads 100 at a time. **Load more** at the bottom brings the next 100.

## Finding transactions

- **Search** matches notes, payee, tags and category name.
- **All / Spent / Earned** narrows by type.
- **Account** and **Category** narrow to one of each.
- **Clear** appears when a filter is on and resets them all.

## Editing and deleting

Click a row to edit it. The **⋯** menu at the end of each row has **Edit**, **Duplicate** (a new entry with the same details, dated today) and **Delete**.

- **Editing** a transaction reverses its previous effect on balances and applies the new values, so your accounts stay correct no matter how much you change.
- **Deleting** a transaction removes it right away and shows **Transaction deleted** with an **Undo** button. Click **Undo** within 6 seconds to bring it back. After that, the delete is final and its balance effect is reversed.

Press **Ctrl+N** (**⌘N** on Mac) anywhere to add a transaction. See [[Tips and Best Practices]] for all shortcuts.

## Tips

- Record transactions soon after they happen. The habit is what makes the rest of the app trustworthy.
- Use the **payee** field consistently (same spelling each time) so search and history stay clean.
- Lean on **tags** for cross-category grouping — for example tag every work-trip expense with `business-trip` regardless of whether it was Transport, Food, or Travel, then search the tag later.
